Tutorial

To find your color season, simply use this on the clothes in your favorite pictures of yourself. Natural soft light encouraged!

Some clothing pieces may have multiple color seasons or have models in different lighting so you may want to test the shadow and light parts then average it out based on their positions on the wheel and your own judgement.

Do not chase perfection. Great and Good can have you find some of your favorite pieces and add diversity to your wardrobe.

Neutrals are categorized by Pure, Half, and Near.

Below are brief explanations of what the ranks mean:

PERFECT - This walked in, stole the spotlight, and left no crumbs!
GREAT - Prepare to turn heads.
GOOD - A quiet win.
OKAY - It is alright but do not expect fireworks.
EH - Not the worst, but definitely not it.
BAD - A total miss, Next!
