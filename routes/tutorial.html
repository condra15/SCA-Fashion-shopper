How to Use Color Season Finder

Once you find your color season, set your color season on the homepage using the dropdown menu which will be automatically saved. To find the color season of any clothes you pick, click the Find Color Season button. This will turn your mouse into an eyedropper that can reach any part of your screen. Click on the area of the item best reflects their color in neutral lighting. And as simple as that you now know how close to a match that color is to your season.

Tips: Some clothing pieces may have multiple color seasons or have models in different lighting so you may want to test the shadow and light parts then pay attention to how close to each other the results are on the season wheel. Do not chase perfection. Great and Good can help you find some of your favorite pieces and add diversity to your wardrobe.

Neutrals are categorized by Pure, Half, and Near.

PURE NEUTRAL - Perfect on everyone
HALF NEUTRAL - Better on some, good on most
NEAR NEUTRAL - Perfect on some, okay at worst

Match explanations:

PERFECT - This walked in, stole the spotlight, and left no crumbs!
GREAT - Prepare to turn heads.
GOOD - A solid win
OKAY - It is alright but do not expect fireworks
EH - Not the worst, but definitely not it
BAD - A total miss
