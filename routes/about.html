Finding Your Color Season

There are 2 methods to determine your color season. The proven method to determine your general color season is through color chroma, brightness, and skin warmth.

Note: Chroma refers to the intensity/purity of a color. If it is pure/intese, it is saturated. If it is duller/less intense, it is muted. If you want to visualize this or learn further google color chroma.

Method 1:
1. Determine if you look better in gold or silver jewelry to fidn your skin warmth. If gold, you're warm and lean towards autumn or spring. If silver, you're cool and lean towards summer or winter. NOTE! If you cannot tell or can kinda do both but you lean towards one more, you fall in between seasons which is good.
2. Look at the table below. Of your favorite clothes in your wardrobe or compliments you've gotten that aren't black, white, or grey, which pattern do they fall in that lines up with your two choices above and the characteristics of the table below? If you line up with two of these seasons, those are the seasons you are between.
3. For the color wheel on the Home page, if you fit just one season you are the true version of that. If you are between seasons, you are one of the two options between true seasons with your skin warmth deciding which you are. However, both of those seasons work well for you when you use our extension so don't stress it.
