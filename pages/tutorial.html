How to Use

Find the color season of any item on your screen in seconds.

1
Set your season

On the Analyze page, select your color season from the dropdown. This is saved automatically.

2
Click Find Color Season

Your cursor becomes an eyedropper. Click on any color on your screen — a shirt on a shopping site, a swatch, anything.

3
See your results

The extension shows the color's season, hex code, and how well it matches yours. Check the wheel to see all 12 season ratings.

Tip

Some items may have multiple colors or be photographed in warm/cool lighting. Try clicking both the lightest and darkest areas to see how the results compare.

Understanding Neutrals

PURE NEUTRAL Perfect on everyone
HALF NEUTRAL Better on some, good on most
NEAR NEUTRAL Perfect on some, okay at worst
