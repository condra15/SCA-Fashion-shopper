Finding Your Season

Two methods to determine your color season.

Note

Chroma = color intensity. Pure/intense = saturated. Duller = muted.

Method 1 The Jewelry Test

Step 1: Do you look better in gold or silver jewelry? Gold = warm (Autumn/Spring). Silver = cool (Summer/Winter). Can't tell? You may fall between seasons.

Step 2: Of your favorite clothes (not black/white/grey), which pattern below matches your warmth?

Step 3: Fit one season = True version. Between two = your skin warmth decides. Both work great with this extension.

Method 2 Use This Extension

Step 1: Find pictures of clothes you've gotten lots of compliments on.

Step 2: Use the Analyze tab to eyedrop those clothes in neutral lighting (or on the store's website) to find their color season. The pattern reveals yours.
